About Black Masks

Founded by Beth Turner · ISSN 0887–7580

Black Masks (ISSN 0887–7580) is a publication on Black entertainment, particularly Black theatre and other Black performing, literary and visual arts. The articles in each issue are largely based on original interviews and research. Every issue also contains an arts hotline that provides current Black entertainment news about events across the country. Black Masks is published quarterly.
